        📊 Why This Platform Is 10x Easier Than SAP Ariba
        
      

      

        
A direct comparison demonstrating why this prototype provides a radically simplified user experience while
          maintaining 100% SAP enterprise compliance:


        

          
            

              Workflow Stage
              Traditional SAP S/4HANA + Ariba (35+ Clicks)
              This Platform (3 Clicks / Excel Backed)
            

          
          
            

              1. Requisition Ingestion
              Requires SAP Cloud Connector, BTP CIG mappings, custom ABAP RFCs, and 15
                configuration steps.
              Transparent Excel Sheet / Clean Core REST: Reads directly from
                SAP_PR_Inbound.csv or OData without touching core ABAP.
            

            

              2. Sourcing Project Creation
              Navigating 8 nested tabs (Summary, Rules, Team, Suppliers, Content, Envelopes)
                taking ~45 minutes.
              Instant Sourcing Cockpit: 1-click import from Requisition,
                automatically setting up the 5-stage stepper and evaluation matrix.
            

            

              3. Supplier Bidding
              Vendors must register on SAP Business Network (Ariba Network), pay annual fees,
                navigate confusing sub-menus, and reset passwords.
              Zero-Friction Supplier Portal: Vendors open direct link, fill
                unit tariffs on a clean checklist console, and click Enviar respuesta.
            

            

              4. Award & Follow-on PO
              Manual scenario allocation, separate approval routing, complex IDoc dispatch, and
                high failure rates.
              1-Click Award: Automated lowest-bid scenario recommendation,
                instant follow-on PO creation, and row written back to Excel sheet.

        📖 Alpek Demo Complete Procurement Flow & User Roles Guide
        
      

      

        

          Zero-Ariba Core Architecture:

          The platform completely eliminates SAP Ariba. The entire
            lifecycle (Stages 1 through 11) runs 100% inside our intuitive web application. We integrate with SAP only
            at the two natural boundaries: reading the Inbound PR and sending the Outbound Purchase Order. Strict RBAC
            is enforced in the backend.
        


        

          
            

              User / Persona
              What they see after login
              Platform Abilities & Workflow Stage
              Backend Security & Boundaries
            

          
          
            

              1. Requester / Employee
Carlos Ramirez		
              Their PRs, corrections, approval status, and deliveries
              Stage 1: Creates PR with needed items, quantity, purpose, budget, and required date. Can edit and
                resubmit if returned.
              Cannot view competitor quotes, evaluate bids, or approve spend.
            

            

              2. Dept Manager / Finance
David Vance		
              Assigned approval tasks and relevant budget information
              Stage 2: Checks business need, cost center budget, and details. Approves, rejects, or returns PR for
                correction with comments.
              Cannot publish tenders or submit quotes.
            

            

              3. Procurement Buyer
Valeria Martinez		
              Their RFQs, suppliers, deadlines, quotations workflow and POs
              Stage 3 & 8: Creates RFQ from approved PR, defines terms, deadlines, invited suppliers, recommends
                winning bid, generates PO.
              Full sourcing authority. Cannot authorize final award budget alone.
            

            

              4. RFQ Approver
Elena Gomez		
              Only approval tasks assigned to them / governance group
              Stage 4: Reviews and approves RFQ specifications and terms prior to official publication to suppliers.
              
              Cannot alter pricing or commercial proposals.
            

            

              5. Supplier A & Supplier B
SONDA & INDORAMA		
              Only RFQs invited to that supplier, its quotations and its own POs
              Stage 5 & 10: Logs in separately, asks clarification questions, submits sealed unit tariffs before
                deadline, dispatches shipments.
              Strictly Confidential Isolation: Backend forbids viewing competitor bids or internal
                data.
            

            

              6. Technical Evaluator
Dr. Victor Chen		
              Assigned technical evaluation tasks and permitted information
              Stage 6: Opens technical envelope. Evaluates technical suitability, ISO compliance, and lead times.
              
              Sealed Bidding: Unit pricing and commercial tariffs are completely hidden from
                technical evaluator.
            

            

              7. Commercial Evaluator
Sofia Morales		
              Assigned commercial evaluation tasks and permitted pricing
              Stage 6: Evaluates commercial pricing, savings % vs baseline, and financial payment terms.
              Cannot modify specifications or approve awards.
            

            

              8. Award Approver
Carlos Alberto Fonseca		
              Award review queue, savings audit, and SAP dispatch button
              Stage 7 & 9: Reviews comparative matrix and justification. Approves award and dispatches confirmed PO
                to SAP ERP.
              Only authorized persona capable of triggering the outbound SAP boundary.
            

            

              9. Receiving Team
Mateo Morales		
              Confirmed POs pending delivery and inward logistics queue
              Stage 10: Records Goods Receipt (GRN), partial batch fulfillments, inspection pass/fail, and
                discrepancy notes.
              Cannot modify PO commercial pricing or terms.
            

            

              10. Procurement / Finance
Order Closure		
              Reconciled purchase orders with complete receipts
              Stage 11: Reconciles received vs ordered quantities, verifies exceptions, and formally
                completes/closes the order.
              Seals the order lifecycle.
